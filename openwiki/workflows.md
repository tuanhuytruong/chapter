# Key Workflows

Chapter supports several primary workflows:
1. **Reading & Review**: Users track daily reading, view summaries, and take notes.
2. **AI Reader & Podcasts**: Automated podcast recap generation, text-to-speech, and content synthesis.
3. **Billing & Memberships**: VietQR integration and tier-based entitlements.

Refer to [Reading Engine](/openwiki/reading-engine.md) for details on the reading pipeline.
