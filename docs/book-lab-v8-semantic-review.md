## Diagnosis / reassessment
Separation reduced visible pedagogical leakage and preserved targeted numeric qualifiers/emotions, but broad domain classification alone does not ensure a meaningful source-grounded private mapping. The schema-valid placeholder is semantically empty. Debrief still misreads explicit human evidence even while producing valid per-decision citations and accurate local observations. IDs, evidence substrings and prompt prohibitions do not prove sourceConnection truth. Next design needs semantically meaningful private mapping validation and explicit coverage/contradiction checks against the complete user decision before prose debrief; not another superficial round of similar prompt edits. Current narrow checks cannot claim to solve all emotions or numeric paraphrases.
